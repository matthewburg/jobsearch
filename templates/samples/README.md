# Samples

Your past professional writing, kept exactly as-is. Claude reads these for **voice**, **format**, and **content** to mine into `../profile/`. Copy files here and keep the originals where they are.

- **Resumes:** every version you have, including role-specific ones. Name them `YYYY-MM_<name>.pdf` (or `.docx`).
- `cover-letters/`: past cover letters. **The best source for learning your voice.**
- `recommendations/`: recommendation letters, reviews, kudos.
- Interview questions and notes from past processes, if you have them.

## Inventory
Claude lists what's here and notes anything special about each file (e.g. "written for a government role; that framing was a requirement, not my default").
